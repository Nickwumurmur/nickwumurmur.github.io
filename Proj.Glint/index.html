刪掉的照片還救得回來嗎？
可以。Glint 刪除的照片會進入 iOS「照片」App 的「最近刪除」相簿，30 天內可以復原。
照片會被上傳嗎？
不會。Glint 全程在你的手機本機運作，沒有帳號、沒有伺服器、沒有分析或廣告 SDK。詳見隱私權政策。
免費可以整理幾張？「終身解鎖」是訂閱嗎？
免費可整理 200 張。「終身解鎖」是一次性買斷（非訂閱、不自動續約），購買後永久移除上限。換手機或重裝後可在付費牆按「還原購買」。
為什麼有些相簿或照片沒有出現？
若你在系統權限中只授權「部分照片」，Glint 只能看到被選取的那些。請到「設定 › 隱私權與安全性 › 照片 › Glint」改為「完整取用」。

Support & Contact

Questions, feedback or bug reports: nickwumurmur@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2–3 business days.

Can I recover photos I deleted?
Yes. Photos deleted with Glint go to the "Recently Deleted" album in the iOS Photos app and can be restored within 30 days.
Are my photos uploaded anywhere?
No. Glint runs entirely on your device — no account, no server, no analytics or ad SDKs. See the Privacy Policy.
How many photos are free? Is "Unlock Forever" a subscription?
You can tidy 200 photos for free. "Unlock Forever" is a one-time purchase (not a subscription, no auto-renewal) that permanently removes the limit. On a new phone, tap "Restore Purchase" on the paywall.
Some albums or photos are missing.
If you granted "Selected Photos" only, Glint can only see those. Go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Glint and choose "Full Access".
